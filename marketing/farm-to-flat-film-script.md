# Farm to Flat — product film

# Narrator: macOS "Tara" (en_IN). Each block is one audio file; timings are targets.

## ACT 1 — THE PROMISE (~14s)

v01: Most vegetables reach your kitchen four days after they left the soil. Farm to Flat was built to close that gap.
v02: Order tonight. It is picked in the morning, and it is at your door before breakfast.

## ACT 2 — THE SHOPPER (~46s)

v03: Signing in takes a mobile number and a six digit code. No password, ever.
v04: Your community, your block, your flat — so the bag reaches the right door on the first try.
v05: The catalog is today's harvest. Real names, in English, Hindi or Telugu — palakura, menthikura, gongura — because that is how people actually shop.
v06: Add what you need. The basket follows you as you browse, and it tells you exactly how far you are from the next offer.
v07: Then you choose when. Every community sets its own delivery windows, and each one shows a live countdown to the moment ordering closes.
v08: Pay by card, by UPI, from your wallet, or in cash at the door.
v09: And then you watch it happen — confirmed, packed, on its way, delivered.

## ACT 3 — THE OPERATOR (~40s)

v10: Behind the app is an operations panel that runs the whole day.
v11: Every live order, filterable by status, community and delivery window.
v12: A procurement list that adds up exactly what to buy at the market this morning — with a cost buffer that flags anything bought over budget.
v13: Delivery windows are yours to shape. Add an afternoon run, rename it, re-time it, or drop it — per community, in one place.
v14: And when something is wrong in a bag, the customer photographs it, and it lands here as a complaint waiting on a decision.

## ACT 4 — THE FIELD (~24s)

v15: The same operation fits in a pocket. The staff console carries orders, the buy list, fulfilment and complaints.
v16: At the door, the customer reads out a four digit code. That code is the proof the bag changed hands.

## ACT 5 — CLOSE (~12s)

v17: Harvested after you order. Never from a cold room.
v18: Farm to Flat. Hyderabad.
