# Farm to Flat — premium interaction film. No em dashes: `say` truncates around them.

p01: Most vegetables reach your kitchen four days after they left the soil.
p02: Farm to Flat closes that gap. Order tonight, and it is picked in the morning.
p03: The catalog is today's harvest, named the way people actually shop, in English, Hindi or Telugu. Add what you need, and the basket follows you.
p04: Then you choose when. Every community sets its own delivery windows, and each one carries a live countdown to the moment ordering closes.
p05: Confirmed in a tap, and the kitchen knows before you have put the phone down.
p06: Behind the app is a panel that runs the whole day. Every live order, and the buy list for tomorrow morning.
p07: Delivery windows are yours to shape. Add an afternoon run, rename it, re-time it, or drop it.
p08: And when something is wrong in a bag, the photograph from the doorstep arrives here, waiting on a decision.
p09: Harvested after you order. Never from a cold room.
